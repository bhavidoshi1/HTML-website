About Me

Hi, I'm Bhavi Doshi, a third year student at Northeastern University studying Marketing Analytics and Data Science. I grew up in New York City, where I developed a strong love for playing volleyball. However, my high school was extremely small and didn't even have its own gym, so I was always looking for opportunities to play outside of school and connect with others who shared the same passion. With the help of some friends and my siblings, we discovered a beautiful sand volleyball court on the rooftop of a building overlooking the New York City skyline. What started as a way to get more playing time quickly became something I looked forward to every week. We would meet two to three times a week and spend around three hours playing, laughing, and enjoying each other's company. It became more than just an opportunity to improve my volleyball skills; it was a space where I could spend quality time with the people I cared about while building lasting memories and friendships. Unfortunately, the court closed down a while ago, but we still make an effort to find opportunities to play whenever we can. Looking back, those afternoons on the rooftop remain some of my favorite memories growing up!
